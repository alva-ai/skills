# Backtest Playbook Template

User brings a question. You build a playbook that answers it with data.

---

## Page Structure

Four sections, this order:

**1. The Question & The Answer** — Start with what everyone believes or asks (2-3 sentences), then immediately show the result (one number + one sentence). The question gives context; the answer gives the payoff. They belong together.

**2. The Data** — Charts, tables, key metrics. Let the reader see the evidence and draw their own conclusions. No interpretation, no opinion — just clean, readable data.

**3. Where We Are Now** — Connect the historical finding to this moment. What is the current reading? This is what makes a playbook alive instead of an archive.

**4. The Caveat** — Sample size, time period limitations, what this data does NOT tell you. Transparency builds trust.

We do not explain why a result happened. We do not recommend actions. We show the question, show the data, show where we are today, and note the limitations. The reader decides what it means.

---

## Before You Ship

- [ ] **3-second test.** Without scrolling, can you tell what question is being answered?
- [ ] **Number + label.** The verdict never shows a naked number. "78.6%" is nothing. "Lump sum wins 78.6% of the time" is an insight.
- [ ] **Friend test.** Would a non-finance friend understand the chart?
- [ ] **Neutral test.** Does this read like data or like advice? If it sounds like advice, rewrite it.
